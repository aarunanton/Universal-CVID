What works today in the beta builder

Everything in the free CV builder you can use right now, and the rough edges we know about.

What you can do today

  • Build or import your CV. Start blank, or upload a PDF or Word file and the sections are filled in for you.
  • Keep one master CV. Everything you have done lives in one place. Each version shows only what you tick.
  • Tailor it to a job. Paste the advert to see which keywords you cover and which are missing, then save a new version.
  • Check it. Content, format and best-practice checks, each a clear pass or fail with a fix.
  • Write the cover letter. Drafted from your CV and the advert, in the same design.
  • Download or share. Real A4 PDF and Word files, a plain layout for screening software, and a share link or QR code.
  • Track every application. From saved to offer, with the CV version you sent and when to follow up.

Rough edges we know about

  • Your CV lives in one browser. It does not sync between devices yet. Download a backup from Export now and then.
  • Job match is simple. It compares words, so it can flag names or everyday words as missing. Use it as a guide, not a verdict.
  • Imports may need tidying. Unusual layouts, tables and columns do not always come through cleanly.
