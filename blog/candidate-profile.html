A candidate profile behind your ID

A living profile, so your career is more than a single file.

The idea

A CV is a snapshot cut down to two pages. A profile could hold the fuller picture: the projects, skills and work you are proud of, and what you are looking for next. You would choose what each employer sees.

Where it stands today

The Profile area in the builder already keeps your details and what you are looking for, and every CV draws on it. It stays in your browser and cannot be shared as a profile yet.

What comes next

A shareable profile linked to your Universal CV ID, with you in control of what is public. Like the ID, it needs data stored online, so the privacy page will be updated first.
