How it works

Three steps, about ten minutes

  1. Import or build your CV

    Upload a PDF or Word file, or start blank with a live A4 preview. Your Universal CV ID is created the moment you start.

  2. Tailor it to a job

    Paste the advert, tick the roles and bullets that fit, close the keyword gaps, and draft the cover letter.

  3. Send and track

    Download a PDF or Word file, or share a link or QR code, and log the application so you know what went where.
