Fair by design

We check the document. Never the person.

Every flag comes from a written rule with a reason you can read. Nothing is a mystery score, and you decide what to change.

How we judge a CV →
  • No score, no pass or failYou get a count of fixes and why, never an “ATS score”.Always on
  • Your CV stays on your deviceIt’s read in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.Always on
  • Never flags gaps, age or nationalityThe reasons can be private, so we leave them alone.Always on
  • Every fix can be dismissed“This doesn’t apply to me” removes it, and tells us the rule needs work.Always on
