Everything a modern CV builder does, tied to one ID

We looked at what the leading CV builders compete on and built each feature around your Universal CV ID.

Versions

Tailored copies, same ID

Keep a main CV and a version for each job. Every version carries your ID, so recruiters always know it's you.

Job match

Match score against the advert

Paste a job advert and see which keywords your CV already covers and which are missing. Add them in one click.

CV check

Feedback as you type

Flags weak openers like “responsible for”, bullets without numbers, long lines and missing details.

Share

Link and QR code

Share a full CV link, or a short contact card with a QR code for business cards. No account needed to view.

Design

Four templates, six accents

From a two-column sidebar layout to a plain black version that screening software reads without trouble.

Tracker

Know what you sent where

Track applications from Saved to Offer, and see which CV version went to which company.

Export

Text PDFs, not pictures

PDFs use real text, so applicant tracking systems can read every word.

Portable

JSON Resume import and export

Move your CV in and out using the open JSON Resume standard. Your data is never locked in.

Private

Nothing leaves your device

Everything is stored in your browser. There's no server, no tracking and no sign-up.

How it works

  1. Build your CV

    Fill in your details with a live A4 preview beside you. Your Universal CV ID is created the moment you start.

  2. Tailor it to a job

    Paste the advert, close the keyword gaps, and save it as a new version for that company.

  3. Share and track

    Send a link, a QR code or a PDF, and log the application so you know what went where.
