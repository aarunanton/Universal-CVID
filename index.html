Everything a modern CV builder does, tied to one ID

We looked at what the leading CV builders compete on and built each feature around your Universal CV ID.

Import

Bring your existing CV

Upload a PDF or Word file and the sections are filled in for you. No retyping.

Master CV

Tick what each job needs

Keep everything you've done in one place. Tick the roles, bullets and skills each version shows; the rest stays stored.

Score

Every check, pass or fail

Content, format and best-practice checks: bullets per role, numbers, weak openers, buzzwords, length and more.

Job match

Match score against the advert

Paste a job advert to see which keywords you cover and which are missing. Save the result as a tailored version.

Design

Six templates, full type control

Font, size, spacing, margins, date format, accent colour and an optional photo. A plain version for screening software.

Pages

Real A4 pages, exact PDF

Long CVs flow onto page two with page numbers. The PDF matches the preview and uses real text. Word export too.

Cover letter

A letter for every version

Drafted from your CV and the job advert, in the same design, ready to edit and download.

Share

Link and QR code

Share a full CV link or a short contact card with a QR code. No account needed to view.

Tracker

Know what you sent where

Track applications from Saved to Offer, and see which CV version went to which company.

How it works

  1. Import or build your CV

    Upload a PDF or Word file, or start blank with a live A4 preview. Your Universal CV ID is created the moment you start.

  2. Tailor it to a job

    Paste the advert, tick the roles and bullets that fit, close the keyword gaps, and draft the cover letter.

  3. Send and track

    Download a PDF or Word file, or share a link or QR code, and log the application so you know what went where.
